## Project Overview

This project was developed as part of the 2024 Student Creativity Program in Invention (PKM-KC) and received national project funding. As an Electrical Division Member, I contributed to the development of a hybrid UAV designed to support reforestation in extreme and difficult-to-access terrain through the seed bombing method. 

My responsibilities included designing and validating a custom Power Distribution Board to provide stable power to the flight controller and propulsion system, integrating the seed bombing actuator with the UAV's electrical system, developing wiring schematics, implementing overcurrent protection, and troubleshooting electrical issues to improve system reliability during mission and flight testing.
