## Project Overview

This project was developed as part of the 2025 Student Creativity Program in Invention (PKM-KC) and received national project funding. As Team Leader and a member of the Electrical Division, I led the development of an autonomous firefighting drone prototype designed to detect and extinguish fires in high-rise buildings using computer vision. 

I designed and integrated the electrical system connecting the flight controller, extinguisher actuator, and computer vision hardware, while also coordinating hardware-software integration, system troubleshooting, and technical preparation to support safe flight testing.
