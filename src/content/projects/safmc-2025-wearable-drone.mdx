## Project Overview

This project was developed during my second year with Cakrawala Skala for the Singapore Amazing Flying Machine Competition 2025. As Team Co-Leader and Head of the Electrical Division, I also served as a technical mentor, guiding the development team in building a more advanced and reliable wearable-based drone control system. 

I supported key design decisions, hardware-software integration, and troubleshooting throughout the development process. Major improvements included upgrading the control interface from a single-glove system to a dual-glove configuration and replacing Wi-Fi communication with the CRSF protocol to improve latency, connection stability, responsiveness, and ease of operation. 

These developments resulted in greater control precision and a smoother user experience, contributing to the team's achievement of 2nd Place in the D1 Man-Machine category at SAFMC 2025.
